Circuit Pocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Circuit Pocket does not collect, store or share any personal data.

All calculations happen on your device. The app makes no network connections and contains no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, so no data is shared with any third party.

The only thing the app saves is your list of favorite tools. It stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to delete, export or withdraw consent for. If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page.

Contact: circuitpocket@icloud.com
