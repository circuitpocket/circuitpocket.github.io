Circuit Pocket

Electronics calculator for iPhone and iPad

Circuit Pocket brings twelve electronics calculators together: Ohm's law, series and parallel resistance, voltage divider, battery life, resistor color code, SMD resistor code, capacitor code, LED resistor, RC time constant, reactance and resonance, 555 timer and op-amp gain. It works offline and collects no data.
